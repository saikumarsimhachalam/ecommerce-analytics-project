\# E-Commerce Sales, Customer Behavior \& A/B Test Analysis



\## Project Overview



This project analyzes e-commerce sales, customer behavior, product performance, city-level performance, and the results of an A/B test.



The analysis was completed using MySQL for data querying and Python, pandas, and Matplotlib for analysis and visualization.



\## Business Problem



The business wanted to understand:



\- Which products and categories generate the most revenue

\- Which customers and cities contribute the most sales

\- Where customers drop off in the purchase journey

\- How cancellations and refunds affect revenue

\- Whether a new website variant improves conversion and purchases

\## Key Findings



\- Total net revenue: ₹1,113,147

\- Total orders: 1,800

\- Average order value: ₹618.42

\- Top product: Food item 16

\- Top category by revenue: South Indian

\- Strongest city: Chennai

\- Visit-to-order conversion rate: 98.6%

\- A/B variant improved conversion rate by 1.22% and purchase rate by 2.53%
